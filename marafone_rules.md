 **Marafon** , **Maraffa** or **Beccaccino** is a [trick-taking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trick-taking "Trick-taking") [card game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Card_game "Card game") for four players from the Italian province of [Romagna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romagna "Romagna") that is similar to [Tressette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tressette "Tressette"), but features [trumps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_(card_games)) "Trump (card games)").^[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marafon#cite_note-pagat-1)^

## Rules

The game is played with a deck of 40 [Italian-suited cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian-suited_cards "Italian-suited cards"), ranked 3 2 A K C J 7 6 5 4 when determining the winner of a trick. In terms of points aces are worth a full point, while deuces, treys and court cards are worth ⅓ of a point; all other cards are worth no points. Each hand is composed of 10 tricks, at the end of a hand the points won are rounded down to a whole number and the winner of the last trick is awarded 1 point. The match continues until a team reaches 41 points.^[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marafon#cite_note-pagat-1)^

Players split into two teams, with teammates sitting at opposite sides of the table. The dealer shuffles the deck and the player to his left cuts it. Ten cards are then dealt to each player, in batches of five.^[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marafon#cite_note-pagat-1)^

In the first hand of a match, the player holding the 4 of coins (or diamonds, if playing with French-suited decks) *christens* the trump suit, called *trionfa* and leads the first trick. In all following hands the player sitting to the dealer’s right will christen the trionfa.^[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marafon#cite_note-pagat-1)^

If a player holds a *cricca* (ace, deuce, trey) of the trionfa they may declare it for an award of 3 bonus points.^[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marafon#cite_note-pagat-1)^

Players must follow suit if they can, and may therefore only play a trump only if they don’t own any card of the leading suit. The trick is awarded to the player of strongest card in the leading suit if no trump was played, or to the player of the strongest trump otherwise. The winner of the trick must lead the next trick.^[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marafon#cite_note-pagat-1)^

At the end of each hand, the points are tallied and the player who led the first trick becomes the new dealer.

It is not allowed to talk during the game.

At the beginning of each trick, the player opening with the first card (i.e. the player who won the last trick or whose christened the trionfa) can declare one of the three coded signals:

- "busso": inviting the partner to win the trick and start the next one with the same leading suit. Usually it means the first player has the second strongest card of the suit.
- "striscio": player still has cards of the same suit in hand (bluf is not allowed). Usually warn the partner that opponents might have no more cards of the leading suit and there is an higher chance of them playing trump card.
- "volo": player has no more cards of the same suit in hand (bluf is not allowed). Usually tells the partner that I can play trump next time the leading suit is this one. Usually "volo" is not declared without trump cards in hand.
